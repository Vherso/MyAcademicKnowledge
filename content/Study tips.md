# P R E G R A D E 
## Prepare
- Read diapo before lectures.
	- So, ideas jump right into your mind.
- Same for Texte → Be effective !!! Read the start and end of the texte, and then you’ll have a better idea of what you’re trying to analyse !!!
## Reading 
- Learn to read fast
	- Méthode 1 : 
		- Read the beginning and the end of a chapter.
			- Then, use your périphérique vision to read every 3 words and let your périphérique vision works. (5:00 for maths)
				- Then, you can take more words at once.
			- “ When you read, you cannot understand it” → after each paragraphes, just ask yourself “What is it about ?”
