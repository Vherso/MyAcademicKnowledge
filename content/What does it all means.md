# 4. The mind-body problem (27-37)
>[!success] Let’s forget about scepticism. The world exist.
> > “ I’ll assume you’re conscious if you assume I am. ”

Evidence : for smthg to happen in our mind/consciousness, *smthg has to happen in our brain*.

> [!tip] Mind *diff from* Brain ?
> - or Mind *is* Brain ?

>[!tip] Can’t observe in your brain, the experience of you *tasting chocolate*.
>> Unobservable by anyone else.


>[!quote] There has to be more to you than your body with its humming nervous system.
>=)  <3


>[!tip] **Dualism** → We’re made up of *2* very diff. things : 
>- Complexe *Physical organism*.
>- A *Soul* which is purely mental.


>[!tip] **Physicalism** → Our *Physical* organism is *sufficient by itself* to give rise to mental life.


They believe that mental states *are* just *states of the brain*, and that there’s no philosophical reason to think they can’t be.
- *Need Science* to prove that.
	- They think it’s just a *matter of time*. (Science have already discovered what light is, how plants grow, how muscles move,…)


---
**Argument (Cont.)** :
- Why Should it be ?
	- >[!quote] Philosophy can’t tell us what stars or diamonds are made of, so how can it tell us what people are or aren’t made of ?

---

**Theory offered in defence of Physicalism** :
- the mental nature of your mental states consists in their relations to the things that cause them and things they cause. 
	- ex: stub your toe,…
		- Could be a purely physical state of your mind.

**Argument (Cont.)** :
- But they also *feel* a certain way.[^3]

**Dualist** thinks *experience* cannot be describe like we describe atoms.
>[!quote] *to discover* that tasting chocolate was really just a brain process, we would have to *analyse something mental* – not an externally observed physical substance but an *inner taste sensation* – in terms of parts that are physical.

A *physical whole* can be *analysed* into *smaller physical parts*. 
- A *mental process can’t be*.

>[!tip] **Dual aspect theory** → Mental life goes on in our brain, BUT our experiences, feelings, thoughts, and desires are *not physical processes* in our brain.
>- the grey mass in our brain is *not just physical object*.

note[^2]

Our brain would still have an *inside that could not be reached by an outside observer*.
